# Home (EN `/`, ES `/es/`)

Scope: the whole single-page portfolio, both locales. Visitor mode: Experience (the shipped work leads), with a Persuade close (contact).
Audience: recruiters and freelance clients equally. Action: contact by email/LinkedIn/GitHub. Proof: live product screenshots and real CV data only.
Constraints: static Astro, self-hosted fonts, no WebGL, reduced-motion and mobile fall back to vertical flow.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is an engineering index: a chaptered, numbered document of shipped work that scrolls like a film reel. It refuses the sidebar-bento developer card grid.
OWN-WORLD: Graphite ground (#121212) with faint 12-column hairlines, bone text (#ECE8DF). Each chapter owns a solid band color: signal orange, cobalt, lime, sodium yellow, with ink-black type on the bands. Bricolage Grotesque at monumental sizes, Geist for reading, JetBrains Mono for bracketed indices, dates and chips. Square corners, 1px rules, no shadows, no glow.
STORY: In 30 seconds a recruiter knows who, where, what stack and what shipped; in 5 minutes an engineer sees how he works. Every chapter ends one move away from contact.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with monogram, numbered nav and EN/ES. A mono terminal prompt `andres@cali:~$ whoami` types once, then the name at ~16vw across the full width, role line and availability pill beneath, two actions (email, LinkedIn) bottom-left, photo cropped small bottom-right with coordinates.
FORM: Pinned chaptered scroll with a horizontal showcase reel; user-pinned fusion of imflorea, kaveenk and ludovicargenty; seed key 6d3ae4a8 (brief overrides the roll).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
